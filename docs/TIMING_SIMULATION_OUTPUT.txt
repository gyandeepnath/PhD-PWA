================================================================================================
THE FOUR CELLS — illumination x sitting order (they are orthogonal by design)
================================================================================================
  Illumination order is counterbalanced against sitting order, so "the dim sitting" is
  the FIRST visit for half the sample and the SECOND for the other half. The two factors
  act on different things: illumination on reading rate, sitting order on setup burden.
  The second sitting skips PARTICIPANT_PROFILE and COLOR_VISION because their predicates
  read participant-scoped fields; consent and both baselines repeat because theirs are
  session-scoped.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  reading exposure per condition      : 177s
  incomplete-blink ratio at p=0.16: SE = 0.059  (95% CI width +/-0.116)
